ABC Syntax:
Notes: C D E F G A B (uppercase = octave 4)
Higher octave: c d e (lowercase), c' d'' for more
Lower octave: C, D, (comma), C,, for more
Accidentals: ^C sharp, _E flat, =F natural
Lengths: C2 half, C4 whole, C/ eighth, C/4 sixteenth
Chords: [CEG]2 (strum together)
Bars: | || |] :| |:
Rests: z

!h! hammer-on · !p! pull-off · !s! slide
!b! bend · !v! vibrato · !pm! palm mute
